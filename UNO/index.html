HOW TO PLAY
1
Match the top discard card by color or number. First player to empty their hand wins!
2
Skip (⊘) — next player loses their turn. Reverse (⇌) — play direction flips.
3
Draw Two (+2) — next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
4
Wild (★) — play anytime, choose any color. Wild Draw 4 (+4) — next player draws 4 and loses turn.
5
If you can't play, draw one card. If it's playable you may play it immediately.
6
When you have 1 card left, shout UNO! (the game shows this automatically).
