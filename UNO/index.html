UNO Rules
  • Match the top discard by color or number. First to empty their hand wins.
  • Skip (⊘) — next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse (⇌) — play direction flips.
  • Draw Two (+2) — next player draws 2 and loses their turn.
  • Wild (★) — play anytime, choose any color.
  • Wild Draw 4 (+4) — next player draws 4 and loses their turn.
  • If you can't play, draw one card. If playable, you may play it immediately.
